About ApexCorp Solutions

ApexCorp is an enterprise software platform dedicated to empowering teams with modern workplace tools. Our management portal delivers high security with token-based authentication (JWT) and clean role segmentation:

  • Employees: Easily log in to view company administrator contacts and your own profile data.
  • Admins: Manage the full directory of company employees, oversee departments, and monitor workforce roles.

Built with high-performance Python FastAPI, robust database storage, and a responsive modern web design.
