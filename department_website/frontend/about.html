About Our Department

The Department of Computer Science is dedicated to providing quality education in computer science, software development and emerging technologies.

Our department focuses on both theoretical knowledge and practical skills so that students can solve real-world problems using technology.

Our Vision

To become a center of excellence in computer science education, innovation and research.

Our Mission

To develop technically skilled, innovative and responsible professionals through quality education and practical learning.

  • Research Facilities
